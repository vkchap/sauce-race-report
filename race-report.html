What this mod does

  • It watches for the start of an event you are riding and records it by itself, from the gun to the finish line. By default it then keeps watching for two minutes to see the riders behind you come in, and describes that separately under "After the line": every number about your own race still stops at the line. "Start now" and "Stop" are there for when you want to record something else, or when an event starts in a way Sauce did not see. A recording you start by hand keeps running until you press Stop: it is not stopped by an event finishing.
  • It keeps your own numbers second by second, and the shape of the race around you: the size of your group, who was in it, who was in the groups ahead of and behind it, and the gaps. A rider only counts as having changed group once the change has held for a few seconds, because Sauce's groups can flicker in a big field.
  • It counts the seconds it actually captured against the seconds the race ran, and says so in the report. A recording with holes in it, or one that started after the gun, is marked incomplete rather than presented as a whole race.
  • It writes the report itself. No AI is involved unless you press one of the two copy buttons and paste the result somewhere yourself. "Copy race commentary for AI (to share)" asks for the race told as a story, for team mates. "Copy coach's debrief for AI" asks for a coach talking you through what your efforts cost and which racing choices to try, and adds a summary of up to three of your earlier races.

Settings

About the data in this report

  • This mod runs inside Sauce for Zwift and reads only what Sauce already has. It keeps everything on this computer. The report window makes no network requests of its own.
  • A race report does not work without knowing who raced, so this mod holds and shows other riders the way Sauce itself does: the rider id and the name Sauce already shows in its own Nearby and Events windows, and the team tag Sauce reads out of that name and shows in its Groups window. It stores no avatar, no country, no weight and no FTP for anyone but you. It never asks Zwift for another rider's profile.
  • Sauce keeps an opt-out list of riders and drops them from the data before a mod sees them. On some Sauce versions that drop is known to remove the wrong riders from a packet, so an opted-out rider can still reach a mod, always without a name. This mod never shows a rider id on screen and never saves one for anyone Sauce could not name: they appear as "Rider A", "Rider B" and so on. Those labels are good for one race only and mean nothing in any other report.
  • "Get official results" is the one button that reaches Zwift. It makes the same call Sauce's own Events window makes when you open a finished event, on your own Zwift login, and Sauce saves every entrant's profile to its own athlete database as a result. Nothing calls it unless you press it.
  • Recordings are kept on this computer, in the storage Sauce gives its windows for this Sauce profile, which every other enabled mod can read. They stay with that profile: cloning or exporting a Sauce profile does not copy them. Use "Save this race to a file" for anything you want to keep, and "Delete" for anything you do not.
  • Nothing here is uploaded anywhere. If you press either copy button, the facts go to your clipboard and it is your choice where you paste them. Anything you paste into an AI service goes to that service under their terms. Rider names are in that text unless you untick the box next to the buttons.
  • Mods are written by third parties. Use at your own risk.

What it cannot tell you

  • Why anyone did anything. Nothing in the data marks intent.
  • Your time gap to the race leader. Zwift sends it and Sauce does not keep it.
  • Riders more than about fifteen minutes ahead of or behind you.
  • Which powerup another rider was holding, or which frame or wheels anyone was on.
  • Anything at all about an event tagged hidethehud or nooverlays. Sauce records nothing for you in those, so no mod can report on them.

Which Sauce this needs

Sauce - or newer. On an older build the data feed this mod uses may not exist, and the window will say so rather than waiting for ever.
